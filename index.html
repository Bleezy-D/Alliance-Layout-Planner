Mouse wheel zooms. Pan with the Pan tool (H) or middle-drag. Click once to place, keep clicking to place more.

Double-click a member city to set the player's name (shown in the square). Double-click an HQ to rename its alliance. Select a trap to see march times from every member city.

Drag a box over buildings to select them all (hold Ctrl or Shift to keep the previous selection and add more, box after box), then Group to make them move as one; clicking any grouped building selects the whole group; erasing a grouped building erases the whole group; Ungroup to edit individually. To pan, use the Pan tool (H) or middle-drag.
