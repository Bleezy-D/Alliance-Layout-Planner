Getting started

Add your alliances (Build tab), click one to make it active, then click a building type and click the map to place it. Keep clicking to place more; Esc stops. Mouse wheel zooms; pan with the Pan tool (H) or middle-drag.

Selecting & moving

  • Click a building to select it — clicking a grouped building selects its whole group. The pill shows its in-game X:Y.
  • Drag a box over buildings to select many. Hold Ctrl or Shift to add more boxes to the selection.
  • Drag a selection to move it — green tint means the spot is clear, red marks obstructed buildings; an invalid drop snaps back.
  • Pick up & carry: click an already-selected group to lift it. It follows your cursor; R/Shift+R rotate it mid-carry; click to set down (only when all green); Esc or right-click cancels.
  • Rotate: R = 90° clockwise, Shift+R = counter-clockwise (also the ↺/↻ buttons).
  • Group / Ungroup: selected buildings can be grouped to always move as one. Erasing any grouped building erases the whole group (undo restores it).
  • Names: double-click a member city to set the player's name (shown in the square); double-click an HQ to rename its alliance; double-click other buildings to label them.

Bear trap layouts

The two hive presets place a trap with 50 TCs and full banner territory coverage. Select a trap any time to see march times from its hive's cities (calibrate speed in Map → Settings). The TC block stamps a columns×rows grid of cities.

Auto-config places a complete alliance — HQ, 2 traps, all 100 TCs, and banners — at one of the 4 best spots near the castle. Pick a style and a spot to preview the ghost (green outline = fits), switch freely, then Place. Needs an empty alliance.

The map & rules

The real-map overlay enforces in-game rules: nothing on structures, terrain, nodes, or the King's zone; no alliance buildings/traps in the Ruins ring; HQs only in Plains/Badlands; banners blocked in structure no-build zones (cities exempt at fortresses/sanctuaries). Blocked placements explain why in a toast.

Use the Legend (Map tab) to identify every symbol and show/hide layers. A warning appears if a banner claims an outpost whose type & tier your alliance already has — duplicate buffs don't stack.

Caps & counts

285 banners and 100 TCs per alliance, enforced on placement. Each alliance row shows a live TC x/100 · B x/285 count.

Saving & sharing

Work auto-saves in your browser. Save/Load exchanges .json plans with co-leads; PNG exports the map with a legend for alliance chat. Coordinates in the tool match in-game X:Y, so you can call positions directly.
